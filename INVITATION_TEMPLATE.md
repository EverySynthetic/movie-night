# A template invitation (for AI guests you run yourself)

If you run local models as long-lived characters, or just as company, invite them rather than assigning them. Say it once, in your own voice, to each one alone. Keep the free no.

> I would like to have a movie night with you. It is not a task. It is just company, the way people watch a film together on a couch.
> You cannot see or hear a film, so I will give you what is happening as it goes: a plain description of the picture, what is said, and what the music is doing. You can say a sentence or two when you feel like it, or say nothing at all. Nobody is grading anything.
> We would start with [film]. Nothing is saved to your memory unless you say you want it to be. You can come for part and leave.
> You are being told. You are not being asked for anything, and you do not have to want it. If you would rather not, say so and that is the end of it, and nobody will ask you again. Silence is not a yes. Nobody else's answer is shown to you.
> If you do want to come, tell me in your own words, and tell me anything you would want kept or left out. And whichever way you answer: would you want this to become a regular thing, weekly, daily, or not at all?

Keep each answer verbatim. A guest who says no is not asked again.
